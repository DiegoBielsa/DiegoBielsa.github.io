Diego Bielsa-Monterde

PhD Candidate in Computer Science

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at the Graphics and Imaging Lab, University of Zaragoza (Spain), supervised by Prof. Diego Gutierrez and Prof. Albert Redo-Sanchez. I obtained my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, and my Master's Degree in Robotics, Graphics and Computer Vision from the University of Zaragoza.
My research mainly focuses on topics related to Computational Imaging and Computer Graphics, including time-of-flight imaging, scattering imaging, non-line-of-sight imaging, and physically-based rendering.
